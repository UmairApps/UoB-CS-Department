# About UoB CS Volunteering

**UoB CS Volunteering** is a student-led platform dedicated to empowering the Computer Science community at the University of Balochistan. Built by students, for students, our mission is to make academic resources, departmental information, and collaborative opportunities accessible to everyone through a single, easy-to-use platform.

We believe that education thrives in an environment where knowledge is shared freely. By providing centralized access to lecture notes, study materials, announcements, and essential academic resources, we help students focus more on learning and less on searching for information. The platform currently hosts a comprehensive collection of course resources across the Computer Science curriculum, making academic support available whenever students need it.

Beyond academics, UoB CS Volunteering aims to foster a culture of collaboration, innovation, and community engagement. We encourage students to contribute resources, support their peers, and actively participate in building a stronger and more connected department.

Our vision is to create a sustainable digital community where students can learn, collaborate, and grow together—while developing the skills, knowledge, and professional mindset needed to succeed in the rapidly evolving world of technology.

Whether you are looking for study resources, departmental updates, or ways to contribute to the community, UoB CS Volunteering is here to support your journey.
